PASS 6 — GAMEPLAY BUGFIXES + PLAYER TURNS
=======================================================

[FIX] Long word overlapped/hid team-name bar
  - Root cause: flex min-height:auto let a long wrapped word expand the
    yellow card beyond the viewport; with justify-content:center the
    overflow ate the top edge, pushing the team bar off-screen.
  - #screen-play -> justify-content:flex-start (team bar pinned top)
  - min-height:0 on .play-land / .pcentre / .wcard so flex children
    shrink instead of forcing overflow
  - .wtext: smaller max clamp (44px), overflow-wrap:anywhere,
    max-height:100% + hidden-scroll so giant words stay inside the card
  - team-bar: nowrap + ellipsis, z-index raised, slightly bolder
