Thank you for reaching out, and for the detailed review. Please find our answers below.

1. Scope of use
Yes — at the moment we intend to use Xsolla products and solutions exclusively for our game "Wham Bam" (https://whambam.games). Wham Bam is a party guessing game for groups, playable directly in the browser, offering three game modes within the single title: "Up Top" (forehead word-guessing), "Charades", and "Don't Say It" (describe the word while avoiding forbidden words). We have no other titles planned for Xsolla integration at this time; if that changes, we will notify you and follow the appropriate process.

2. Subscriptions — what they include
Our subscriptions grant access to premium digital content within the game only:

- Premium word decks: the game ships with a set of free decks; a subscription unlocks the full catalog of premium themed word decks (e.g., Disney & Pixar, Superheroes, Music Artists, Bollywood, and others — currently 24 decks totaling over 15,000 words).
- Expanded word pools: subscribers receive the full word pool for each deck rather than the limited free selection.
- An "All Access" tier that unlocks every premium deck for the duration of the subscription.

All subscription content is consumed entirely inside the game. Nothing purchasable has any real-world monetary value, cannot be transferred between users, and cannot be withdrawn or redeemed outside the game.

3. Relation of the applicant to the project
Rohini Paralkar is a business partner and co-founder of the project, and submitted the application on behalf of the team.

4. Tournaments and prizes
We do intend to organize casual in-game tournaments (e.g., leaderboard-based score competitions between players and teams). To be clear about the nature of the prizes: any prizes will be usable exclusively within the game — for example, temporary or permanent unlocks of premium word decks, subscription time, or cosmetic recognition such as leaderboard badges. Prizes will never be withdrawable, convertible to cash or cryptocurrency, transferable, or exchangeable for any form of value outside the game. No entry fees will be charged for tournaments.
