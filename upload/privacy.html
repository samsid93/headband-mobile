Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026
Draft for legal review. This document was prepared as a working first draft covering how the app actually behaves. It has not been reviewed by a qualified lawyer. Please have it checked against your jurisdiction and your final payment and analytics arrangements before you rely on it or submit the app to Apple or Google.

This policy explains what information Wham Bam collects, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies to the game at whambam.games and to our mobile apps.

1. The short version

  • You can play the entire game without an account and without giving us any personal information.
  • If you choose to create an account, we store your email address, your display name and your game scores.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it at any time.

2. What we collect

If you play as a guest

Nothing that identifies you. Your chosen game settings, unlocked decks and local score history are stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. We cannot see them, and clearing your browser data erases them.

If you create an account

InformationWhy we need it
Email addressTo create and secure your account, confirm it is yours, and let you reset your password
Display nameTo show who you are on the leaderboard
PasswordStored only as a cryptographic hash — we never see or keep the password itself
Game resultsScores, rounds played and decks used, so your progress and leaderboard position follow you between devices

If you sign in with Google

We receive your email address, your name and your profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we have no other access to your Google account.

If you buy a subscription

Payments are handled entirely by our payment providers. Card numbers never reach our servers. We store only the fact that a subscription is active and when it expires, so we know what to unlock for you.

Technical information

Our hosting provider records standard server information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the service running.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Local storage keeps your game settings, unlocked decks and offline score history on your device. It never leaves your device.
  • A sign-in session is stored on your device if you log in, so you stay logged in between visits.
  • Security cookies may be set by Cloudflare to protect the site from automated abuse. These are strictly necessary for the service to work.

Where consent is required in your region, we ask before storing anything that is not strictly necessary, and you can change your answer at any time from the game's menu.

5. Children

Wham Bam is a family party game and we expect children to play it. Children can play as guests without giving us anything.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). Accounts are intended to be created by a parent, guardian or an adult player. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. How long we keep things

  • Account information — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Scores and leaderboard entries — until you delete your account, or clear your local history for guest play.
  • Payment records — as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Server logs — a short period, for security purposes.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Traffic to and from the game is encrypted. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers put in place for international transfers.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
